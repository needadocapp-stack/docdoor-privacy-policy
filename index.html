Privacy Policy

📋Introduction

Welcome to DocDoor, operated by Divya Tech. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

By using DocDoor, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the app.

Effective Date: May 21, 2025  ·  Last Updated: May 21, 2026

🗂️Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information when you register and use DocDoor:

Personal Identification

  • Full name – used to identify your account
  • Email address – used for login and notifications
  • Phone number – used for account verification and contact
  • Profile photo – uploaded optionally; stored securely on our servers

Health & Appointment Information

  • Appointment history – doctors visited, dates, and statuses
  • Doctor specialization preferences – specialties you browse or book
  • User role – whether you are a Patient, Doctor, or Administrator

Doctor-specific Information (if you register as a Doctor)

  • Medical specialization, qualifications, and practice details
  • Availability slots and consultation fee

⚙️How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your DocDoor account
  • To facilitate appointment booking between patients and doctors
  • To display your profile to relevant users (e.g., patients viewing doctor profiles)
  • To send appointment reminders and status updates
  • To improve app features and fix technical issues
  • To comply with legal obligations where required

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

🔒Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secured servers. Profile photos and other media are stored using cloud storage with access controls. Authentication tokens are stored locally on your device using Android's encrypted DataStore.

We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption for all data transmitted between the app and our servers. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your password is never stored in plain text. Authentication is handled via secure tokens that expire automatically.

🤝Data Sharing

We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With doctors – patients' names and appointment details are shared with the booked doctor
  • With patients – doctors' professional profiles (name, specialization, photo, fee) are visible to patients
  • Service providers – trusted third-party services (e.g., cloud hosting, Firebase for notifications) that operate under strict data protection agreements
  • Legal requirements – if required by law, court order, or governmental authority

🔗Third-Party Services

DocDoor uses the following third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) – for push notifications
  • Google Play Services – for app distribution and device authentication

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these services. DocDoor is not responsible for the data practices of third-party providers.

✅Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Update or correct your profile information at any time within the app
  • Delete your account and associated data via the Profile screen in the app
  • Withdraw consent by uninstalling the app and requesting data deletion

To exercise any of these rights or to request a copy of your data, contact us at support.docdoor@gmail.com.

👶Children's Privacy

DocDoor is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

🔄Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the Last Updated date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of DocDoor after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
