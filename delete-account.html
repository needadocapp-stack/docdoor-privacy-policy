Account Deletion Guide

🗑️Delete Your Account

DocDoor allows you to permanently delete your account at any time. This guide explains how to delete your account, what happens to your data, and how to contact us if you need assistance.

Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data, appointments, and profile information will be removed from DocDoor.

📱How to Delete Your Account

Method 1: Delete via the App (Recommended)

Follow these steps to delete your account directly from the DocDoor app:

1
Open the DocDoor App

Launch the app on your device and log in with your credentials if needed.

2
Navigate to Your Profile

Tap the profile icon (usually in the bottom navigation bar or top-right corner) to access your profile menu.

3
Find Settings or Account Options

Look for "Settings," "Account," or "Profile Settings" option in the menu.

4
Select Delete Account

Scroll down to find the "Delete Account" or "Remove Account" button (usually in red text or warning section).

5
Confirm Your Decision

A confirmation dialog will appear. Read the warning message carefully, then tap "Delete" or "Confirm" to proceed.

6
Enter Your Password or Verify Identity

You may be asked to enter your password or verify your phone number for security purposes.

7
Account Deletion Complete

You will be logged out and returned to the login screen. Your account is now permanently deleted.

Method 2: Delete via Google Play Store

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion through Google Play Store:

  1. Open Google Play Store
  2. Search for "DocDoor"
  3. Tap on the app → About this app
  4. Tap the ⋮ (three dots) menu
  5. Select "Report an issue"
  6. Request account deletion with your email address
  7. We will process your request within 30 days

Method 3: Contact Our Support Team

You can also request account deletion by contacting us directly:

Email: support.docdoor@gmail.com
Subject: "Account Deletion Request"
Include in your email: Your registered email address and full name

⚠️What Happens When You Delete Your Account

Data Deleted Immediately

  • Your account profile (name, email, phone number)
  • Profile photo and uploaded images
  • Authentication tokens and session data
  • Local app data on your device
  • Device tokens (push notification access)

Appointment Data

  • Future appointments are cancelled
  • Doctors and hospitals are notified of cancellations
  • Past appointment records are anonymized (not deleted for medical record retention)
  • Any consultation fees paid are non-refundable

Data Retention for Compliance

For legal and regulatory reasons, we may retain anonymized records for:

  • Medical and healthcare compliance (medical council regulations)
  • Financial audit trails (payment records, anonymized)
  • Fraud prevention and security
  • Duration: Up to 7 years as per Indian regulations
⚠️ Important: Even after account deletion, anonymized appointment history may be retained for legal compliance. Personal identifiers (name, email, phone) are removed.

❓Frequently Asked Questions

Can I recover my deleted account?
No, account deletion is permanent. Your data cannot be recovered after deletion. However, you can create a new account after 30 days using the same email address.
Will my appointments be cancelled if I delete my account?
Yes, all future appointments will be automatically cancelled, and the assigned doctors will be notified. Patients who booked with you (if you're a doctor) will also be notified of the cancellation.
Will I get a refund if I delete my account?
No, consultation fees and any paid services are non-refundable. However, you can cancel individual appointments before deletion to potentially get refunds based on cancellation policies.
How long does account deletion take?
Account deletion is processed immediately in the app. However, it may take up to 24-48 hours for all associated data to be fully removed from backup systems.
Will my identity verification documents be deleted?
For doctors: Identity verification documents (Aadhaar, medical certificates) are deleted from your profile immediately. However, anonymized records may be retained for 7 years for compliance with medical council regulations.
Can I delete my account if I'm a doctor with active appointments?
Yes, but all your active appointments will be cancelled. Notify your patients beforehand and ensure continuity of their care. Admin staff may contact you to ensure proper handover.
Will my data be shared with third parties after deletion?
No, your personal data will not be shared with anyone after deletion. Firebase and Google services will stop receiving your device tokens immediately.
Can I export my data before deleting my account?
You can request a complete data export by contacting us at support.docdoor@gmail.com. Include "Data Export Request" in the subject line. We'll provide your data in JSON or CSV format within 7 days.
What if I encounter an error during deletion?
If the in-app deletion fails, try the Google Play Store method or contact our support team at support.docdoor@gmail.com. Include your account email and the error message you received.
When can I re-register with the same email?
After 30 days of account deletion, you can register a new account using the same email address. This delay is to prevent accidental re-registration and to ensure complete data purge from systems.

✅Before You Delete – Checklist

Consider these steps before deleting your account:

  • Export Your Data: Request a data export if you need records for medical purposes
  • Cancel Appointments: Individually cancel upcoming appointments to avoid cancellation by deletion
  • Download Receipts: Save any payment receipts or appointment confirmations
  • Notify Doctors: If you're a doctor, inform your patients about closing your practice
  • Request Refunds: Check if any appointments are eligible for refunds under our cancellation policy
  • Archive Important Info: Take screenshots of important appointment or medical history data
💡 Tip: If you're just taking a break, consider deactivating your account instead (if available) rather than permanently deleting it.

🔄Alternatives to Account Deletion

If you're considering deletion, these alternatives might be helpful:

Temporarily Disable Your Profile

Take a break without losing your data. Contact us to temporarily hide your profile or disable notifications.

Change Your Privacy Settings

  • Disable location services
  • Turn off push notifications
  • Opt-out of recommended doctors
  • Hide your profile from public search

Update Your Information

Update your email, phone number, or password to secure your account. You can also remove your profile photo.

Contact Support First

If you have concerns or issues, reach out to us before deleting. We may be able to help!
